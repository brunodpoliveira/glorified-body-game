# 2026-11-16: the T49 models in the game (Lucy, the guard, the patient, the nurse)

Bruno passed the T49 Lucy (round 175) and then the guard, the patient and the nurse, made with the T26 full mesh and
the T27 bake (round 178). In round 178 Lucy's Crown became the separate T27 helmet with its own material. These four
replace the T19 models of the same classes. `lucy_guard` and the other classes come in later batches.

## What changed

- `tools/characters.sh`: the table `ONE_FILE` names a class that comes as one passed game GLB (the rig, the clips and
  the maps). The script reads that file as both source files. Now: `lucy_bare`, `guard`, `patient`, `nurse`.
- `tools/characters_basecolor.py --maps`: one map set for each mesh of the file: the base colour, the normal map and
  Unity's metallic-smoothness map (R = metal, A = 1 - roughness), at 2048. The largest mesh is `<class>`, any other
  mesh is `<class>_<node>` (Lucy's Crown: `lucy_bare_crown_t27`). A T19 class keeps the base colour at 1024.
- `tools/characters_export.py`: keeps every skinned mesh (Lucy has two: the body and the Crown on the head bone).
- `CharacterImportSettings`: `_normal` imports as a normal map, `_ms` as linear data. A class with a map set imports
  at 2048 with high-quality compression; a T19 base colour stays at 1024, uncompressed. `RefreshStaleAvatar`: the
  build maps the Humanoid again when the node names of an FBX change (the old `.meta` kept the T19 reference pose).
- `CreateCharacterAssets`: one material for each map set: the normal map and the smoothness from the map. Lucy's skin
  is 0.25, her gold veins 0.65 at metal 0.9, the cloth of the T27 bake is capped at 0.25, and the hard parts keep their
  gloss. A part with its own maps gets its own material (`lucy_bare_crown_t27.mat`). A T19 class stays fully matte.
  Every prefab carries its character layer (Lucy: Player, the other figures: Enemy). The scenes set the layer node by
  node, so a new mesh node had no override.
- `LucyModelPlaythrough` (bot `lucy`): `tools/unity.sh play Tutorial Main_Menu lucy 300`. It checks Lucy (stand, walk,
  run, the pain clip) and then each round 178 figure (stand, walk).

## Proof

- The exports: Lucy 16,758 triangles (the body 13,958 and the Crown 2,800), the guard 9,000, the patient 8,999, the nurse
  9,000. Each has 23 bones. Lucy's clips stay in place. The walk and the run of the other three travel (walk 1.33 to
  1.50, run 2.54 to 2.88 a cycle).
- `CreateCharacterAssets.BuildAll`: clean, 9 of 9 classes.
- EditMode `CharacterImportTests`: 7 of 7 passed. Smoothness, the median of what is not metal: 0.25 for all four. On
  Lucy, p90 0.25, so no part of the skin is glossy (the neck band too). The guard is glossy on 28 % of the map: the boots,
  the belt, the mask and the cap, all hard parts.
- Bot `lucy` in the Tutorial room: PASS.
  - Lucy: 1.706 m, the feet 1.4 cm over the floor, facing correct. The pain clip (made on the T19 rig) plays: the
    hand is 0.16 m from the head.
  - The guard: 1.808 m, the feet 0.8 cm over the floor. The patient: 1.683 m, 1.1 cm. The nurse: 1.676 m, 1.7 cm. All
    three face their root forward.
