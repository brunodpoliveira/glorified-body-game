## The room

The Gallery of floor 1 (`CONCEPTS.md` §5): 16 x 8 m, 4.5 m high, raw concrete, three planters with tamed
shrubs, two columns, two conduits, a steel door with the gear above it, two more gear panels. Five spots from
the ceiling (hard shadows, warm pools, one cold light at the door), ambient near black, no sun. Lucy at the near
end, a soldier and a guard down the gallery (lock-on targets, no AI). Three fixed cameras inside the room at a
level angle: Cam_Wide (the near corner, the default), Cam_Mid (from the side), Cam_Door (from the far end).
Two zones cut to Cam_Mid and Cam_Door as she walks. The scene: `Assets/_Game/Scenes/LookTest.unity`, built by
`LookTestSetup.BuildLookTest` (menu GlorifiedBody > Build Look Test). The room surfaces are `RoomFace` quads
with the UVs in metres (a 2 m tile repeats as 2 m on every wall).

## The three dressings (F10 cycles, the label at the top left)

| Dressing | What it is | Renderer |
|---|---|---|
| Greybox | flat grey Simple Lit, the stock renderer, no dither | 0 (PC_Renderer) |
| RealTime | the textures, the spots live every frame, 720p scaled up, 32 levels with a Bayer dither on the background | 1 GB_Full |
| Plate | the same room rendered ONCE per camera (ambient occlusion on, 4096 shadow maps) into a plate; each frame draws the plate, the room as depth only, then the characters | 3 GB_Plate (the capture on 2 GB_Expensive) |

The 1999 traits, as built (`CONCEPTS.md` §7):

1. Hard light: five spots with hard shadows, flat ambient 0.015, no GI, no soft shadows (the URP asset).
2. The detail in the texture: Simple Lit (Blinn-Phong, one hard highlight), one colour map, no normal map.
3. The frame at 720p of 1080p (`renderScale` 2/3, linear upscale) in every dressing.
4. The dither on the background only: a RenderObjects feature redraws the characters where visible (depth
   Equal) and writes 1 in the stencil; the full-screen dither pass skips stencil 1. The characters keep their
   colours (Bruno rejected the jitter on characters).
5. The plate: `LookDirector.CapturePlate` renders the room with the camera into a 1280 x 720 texture (render
   scale 1 for that frame, then back) with the expensive renderer; `PlateBlitFeature` blits it before the
   opaques; the room then renders depth only (`Hidden/GB/DepthOnly`) so the planters hide the figures; the
   characters are the only opaques of GB_Plate. A camera cut captures again. Lucy's shadow on the floor is
   lost in the plate (the floor is a picture): the honest cost of the idea.

## Textures: RFab first, then code (RFab gap 11)

`tools/look_textures.sh` asked RFab for the five pictures at the lowest tier. The default image model drew
rooms, a hand with soil and a girl, not surfaces, in 4 of 4 for every surface, at 512 px ($0.19 for 20
pictures, `~/glorified-body-renders/game/look_test/textures/sheet_*.png`). The same prompts on
`seegen:nano-banana-pro` with `--n 1` gave four real surfaces at 1024 px ($1.28, `.../textures/nb/sheet_nb.png`):
the formwork lines and tie holes on the wall, a sealed floor with blotches, brushed steel with a rivet seam,
soil with bark. A plain repeat shows a soft seam (`sheet_nb_tiles.png`), the mirror wrap hides it. The room
uses those four. The gear emblem and a fallback set of all five come from `tools/look_textures_procedural.py`
(periodic value noise, tileable by construction, 1024 px; the emblem drawn as shapes: a white gear on black).
The import: 1024, bilinear, mirror wrap (clamp on the emblem), no compression. Row 11 in `RFAB_GAPS.md`.

## For Bruno (the pick)

1. Real time or plate. On this room both look the same at a glance. The plate buys the expensive pass once
   (ambient occlusion, 4096 shadow maps) for no cost per frame. The plate costs: no character shadow on the
   floor, a 22 ms hitch on each camera cut, and a prop that moves (a door) must be drawn as a character.
2. The darkness. The corners are near black by the ruling. The walls read through the washers. Say brighter
   or darker.
3. The texture scale: 2 m tiles. The formwork lines read as 50 cm bands on the walls.
4. The gear panels: 1.6 m at 2.6 m high (1.2 m over the door). The one by Cam_Wide is in the face of the lens.
5. The textures: RFab Nano Banana (in the room) against the procedural set (`textures/procedural/`).
