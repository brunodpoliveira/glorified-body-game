## What changes

- The 32-level Bayer dither (`Hidden/GB/Dither`, `GB_Dither.mat`) leaves the project, with its full-screen pass in
  `GB_Full`, `GB_Expensive` and the remake's `GB_RemakeDither`.
- The `CharacterStencil` pass goes too. It drew each character a second time to mark it, so that the dither skipped
  it. With no dither it only cost a draw.
- `LookTestSetup.RemoveDither` (menu GlorifiedBody > Rendering > Remove the dither) does the asset part once.
  `LookTestSetup.EnsureRenderers` no longer adds the two passes.
- The LOOK option stays, with two values: "Sharp (no anti-aliasing)" (it was "Authentic (1999)") and "Clear
  (anti-aliasing)". The setting keeps its number, so a saved choice stays.
