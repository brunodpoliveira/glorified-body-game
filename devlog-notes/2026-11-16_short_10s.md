| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The length | 16 s | 10 s (`ShortController.Seconds`) |
| Shocks, one each 0.4 s | 40 | 25 |
| One shock on an enemy | 12 | 19.2 (`ShortController.ShockDamage`) |
| All shocks on the enemies | 480 | 480 (`DamageToOthersTotal`) |
| Her HP loss | half her HP | half her HP |

Her loss was already a share of her HP spread over the shocks, so it needed no change. The length and the shock damage
were serialized fields, and every scene kept its own copy (16 s and 12). A new default would not reach a built scene, so
both are constants now. The freeze at the start (2.5 s) and the stun of each shock do not change. A short that goes on in
the next room keeps what is left of the 10 s.

(10 s, and 480 on the enemies in all).
