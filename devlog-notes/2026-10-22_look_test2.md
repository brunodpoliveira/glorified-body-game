## The four dressings (F3 cycles, F11 loads the next look room)

| Dressing | What it is |
|---|---|
| Greybox | flat grey, the stock renderer |
| RealTime | the textures, the spots live, no bounce (the ruling), 720p with the 32-level dither on the background |
| RealTimeBaked | RealTime plus the baked bounce light: the lightmaps of the room (indirect only, the spots stay live and hard) and the probes for the figures |
| Plate | the room rendered once per camera with the bounce, ambient occlusion and 4096 shadow maps into a 720p plate, the figures drawn over it |

`LookTestSetup.BakeAll` bakes both scenes on the CPU lightmapper (10 texels a metre, two bounces, the ambient
occlusion, no compression): the Gallery in 40 s, LookTest2 in 59 s, two lightmaps each (about 1.6 MB a scene).
`LookDirector` turns the baked light on and off with the dressing: the global lightmap list, the index of every
lightmapped renderer, and the probe use of the room and of the characters. The first pass forgot the room's
probe use: a room renderer without its lightmap falls back to the baked probes, so "real time" carried the bounce
and came out brighter than "baked". Now real time is the ruling: the direct light and a near-black ambient.

## The finding

Without the bounce the corridor and the gym are near black between the pools (`sheet_cam_corridor_s.png`,
`sheet_cam_gym.png`): the walls read only where a spot hits them. With the baked bounce every room reads: the
concrete, the tiles, the ceiling, the figures in the probes. The plate is the baked dressing plus the ambient
occlusion, with the figures over it (no shadow of Lucy on the floor, the cost of the idea). The 1999 games had
their bounce in the pre-rendered backgrounds. The pick is now between three looks, not two.

## The textures (2K, RFab)

`tools/look_textures.sh`: ten surfaces on `seegen:nano-banana-pro` with `--res 2k` ($0.32 each, the same as
1K; the raised tier needs `--tier-reason`), 50 to 110 s a picture, $3.20: the concrete wall with panel seams,
tie holes and stains, the floor with a saw-cut grid, the board-formed ceiling, brushed steel with a rivet row,
soil, white tiles, pale floor tiles, the resin floor, a louvred vent grille, a fern on black (keyed to alpha by
`tools/look_fern_alpha.py`, 1812 px). The gear emblem, the CRT face and the light panel are drawn by
`tools/look_textures_procedural.py` (2K, with a tileable fallback for every surface). Import: 2048, trilinear,
anisotropy 8, no compression, mirror wrap (clamp for the stretched pictures). The relief is in the picture
(the seams carry a shadow), no normal map. 66 MB of PNG in git.
