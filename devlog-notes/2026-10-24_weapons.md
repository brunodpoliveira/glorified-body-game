## The bug: the lock marker

Fact check: the marker was a red sphere at a fixed height (chest 1.2 m + 0.9 m = 2.1 m). The power armour is
2.6 m tall, so the sphere was inside its head. CONFIRMED.

Fix (`LockOnController`): the marker floats 0.3 m over the top of the target's own meshes. The power armour's chest
(its aim point) also scales with its height (1.73 m, was 1.2 m). Test: `LockMarker_FloatsOverThePowerArmoursHead`
(the armour top 2.63 m, the marker 2.93 m).

## What I built

1. **Attack kinds** (`DummyShooter`): the weapon decides the ranged attack.
   - Pistol: one shot, 15 damage.
   - Assault rifle: a burst of 3 rounds, 0.1 s apart, 8 damage each. One dodge clears the whole burst.
   - Grenade launcher: the grenade arcs to the floor under her and bursts after a 0.5 s fuse. Radius 2.5 m, 35
     damage at the centre, half at the edge. A red ring on the floor shows the blast zone from the launch to the
     burst (`EnemyGrenade`). No grenade closer than 3.5 m.
   - Melee: within 1.8 m, any soldier strikes instead of a shot. The strike lands within 2.2 m and 70 degrees of
     his front, 20 damage. The dodge's invulnerability avoids it.
   Every kind takes a difficulty slot, shows the telegraph circle, and waits the same time. The soldier never
   moves during the aim, the circle, the burst or the strike.
2. **The timing**: the difficulty presets carry `attackSeconds` (1.5 / 1.0 / 0.8) and `restSeconds` (2.0). One
   third of the time is the laser, two thirds the circle. The training dummies follow it too.
3. **The shield** (`EnemyShield`, `IFrontGuard`): the vendor great shield, 1.0 m tall, in front of his left side.
   From the front (75 degrees each side) it stops mk_warspite, mk_erebus and returned bullets: no damage, a spark,
   a clang, "BLOCKED" on the HUD, and the MK is spent. The side and the back are open. A lift tears it away (it
   falls to the floor), and a death drops it.
4. **The grenade catch**: the defend press with mk_orzabal catches a grenade in the air within 2.5 m of her chest,
   for 15 MK. It flies back to the shooter and bursts there at once, twice as hard, on the enemies only.
5. **The attack icons** (`PlayerHud`): pistol, rifle with three dots (the burst), grenade, a starburst (melee).
   The icon shows next to the circle from the laser on, and next to the edge arrow when the attacker is out of the
   frame. Bosses add their own icons later (an area attack, a power).
6. **The run**: once alerted, a soldier runs to its spot, and sprints when the spot is more than 8 m away. It turns
   at 16 (was 10). The fastest soldier moved at 3.2 m/s in the test. When she comes within melee range during a
   move, it stops and strikes.
7. **The AI TEST room**: `Soldier_A` carries the assault rifle, `Guard_B` the shield and the pistol, `Armour_C`
   (the power armour) the grenade launcher.

Models and sounds come from the vendor folders (local only, not in git): `AI_vAssaultRifle`, `AI_vShotgun` (the
placeholder launcher), `AI_vGreatShield`, `Explosion_Air`, and the vendor rifle, RPG, slash, punch and block
sounds. All numbers are PROPOSED.

## Later the same night: gas, rockets, frag

Bruno (2026-10-24): "is there gas grenades available? those would justify why the soldiers and guards have
masks, plus it makes sense in-story (they're trying to capture her alive); the power armor may have frag grenades
and rockets". His picks: the gas is poison; a separate gas grenadier class; mk_orzabal catches a rocket for more
MK.

1. **The gas grenadier** (`Gasser_D`, the soldier model, the `GasLauncher`): the gas grenade flies the same arc
   with a GREEN ring and bursts into a poison cloud (`GasCloud`): 2.5 m radius, 6 s, 6 HP per second to her
   while she stands in it. The masked enemies take no gas damage. The cloud is the vendor smoke grenade's smoke,
   tinted green (`Assets/_Game/Effects/GasCloud_Smoke.prefab`, made by the factory, kept out of git: it is a copy
   of vendor data).
2. **The power armour** (`ArmourLaunchers`): a frag grenade, then a rocket, then a frag grenade. The rocket
   (`EnemyRocket`, the vendor RPG missile mesh, an orange trail) flies straight at 10 m/s and bursts on the first
   thing it meets: 2 m radius, 40 damage at the centre.
3. **The catch**: mk_orzabal catches a rocket within 2.5 m for 25 MK. A returned rocket steers to its shooter (a
   straight one missed the running power armour in the test) and bursts on the enemies, twice as hard. Inside
   the catch radius the press takes the bigger threat first: a rocket, then a grenade, then a bullet. (A test
   caught a rifle bullet that was nearer than the grenade.)
4. **Icons**: a cloud (gas) and a rocket join the HUD set.
5. **Placeholders**: the gas grenadier and the power armour carry the vendor shotgun model as their launcher.
