## What changed

### One world scene

- `Scenes/Demo/Demo.unity` holds all 22 rooms and the stair room S1 under "[World]". START loads it once. The 22 room
  scenes (`Demo_W1` and the rest) are gone.
- `DemoWorld.BuildAll` builds it. Each room is a root with a `Room` component (`Scripts/Rooms/`): its id, title, map
  floor, plan rectangle and the rooms behind its doorways. The world has one Lucy, one camera, one light, one camera
  director and one NavMesh.
- A doorway opens straight into the next room. The stub corridors and the room exits are gone. A wall that two rooms
  share stands once, on the room line, as tall as the taller room (`WallLedger`).
- Floor 1 stands 4 m lower and to the south-east (`DemoWorld.Floor1Offset`, 12.5, -4, -31).
- The stair room S1: a 2 m landing, an 8 m ramp down 4 m (26.6 degrees), a 2 m landing at G1. Two cameras. The card
  door of W12 opens onto it.

### Her room

- `RoomTracker` finds her room every frame. A room change raises `RoomTracker.OnChanged`. The things below listen.
- Culling: the shell and the props of a room show only when she is in it or next door. The colliders stay, so a
  soldier who chases her walks on a floor.
- The music takes the key of her room. The same key changes nothing, so a chase keeps its music.
- The map: her room and the rooms she was in (`RoomTracker.Visited`, in the saves). Down the stair it goes to floor 1.
  The atlas reads the world scene (`MapAtlasSetup.ReadWorld`).
- RETRY: when she walks into a room, the save system keeps her state, the world, the flags and the place where she came
  in. A RETRY with no checkpoint reloads the world and puts her there. This is the same "retry at the door" as before.
- A beat that ran "when the room starts" (`runOnStart`) runs when she first enters its room.
- A fate flag ("spared") goes up when she leaves that person's room.
- A figure group behind a flag (`FlagGate.waitUnseen`) does not appear in her view. It waits until she enters its room
  or cannot see it.
- Save version 2. Older saves do not load (their room scenes are gone).

### Walls stop sound and powers

- Alerts, screams and power hits reach her room and the rooms next door, not further (`Room.Hear`). The enemies of
  the next room then chase her through the door (Bruno's pick).
- A death heard with no line of sight, a hider's whisper and a carer's search stay in one room (`Room.Same`).
- A bark two rooms away has no sound and no subtitle. A screen speaks only in its room.
- In the world, lock-on needs a clear line, and the short does not shock through a wall.

### FMV cutscenes and their steps

- FMV worked before: a cutscene shot can be a video. New: the skip key opens a menu (PAUSED, RESUME, SKIP) and the
  clip, the line and the pan hold (`CutscenePlayer.MenuOpen`). Up and down choose, Interact or Enter confirms,
  Escape resumes.
- The steps after a cutscene run after a skip too. New step: `ScriptStep.Give(itemId)` gives her an item (a full
  inventory drops it at her feet).
- The two W1 beats are FMV slots (`DemoScripts.Fmv`): `Resources/Cutscenes/cutscene_01_the_wake` and
  `cutscene_02_the_scientist`. Put the clip at `Assets/_Game/Cutscenes/<id>.webm` (VP8) and build the world again:
  the clip becomes the cutscene. Until then a dark card shows each line of the writers' script as a subtitle.
- The scientist now waits in the corridor W2 behind W1's door. She sees him come into the doorway, the cutscene plays,
  then he steps in and attacks.
