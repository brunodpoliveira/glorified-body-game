# 2026-11-11: the W14 TV, watched as a cutscene

Bruno, after rows 10 and 11 of the manual test (his shot: her text over the speech, mid-show):

```text
the TV is wrong; she is meant to watch the cutscene from where the player started to interact with it and she'll only say this line at the end of the broadcast
```

## The fact check: CONFIRMED

1. The build put her text on the TV table as a common examine line (`DemoNight.W14Tv`). The Interact key showed it at
   any time, also over the speech.
2. The TV had no watch view. Its `InWorldScreen` took no key (`interactive: false`).
3. The canon (round 109, `game/lines/N1_news.md` N2) puts her text "while the credits roll". The build did not hold
   to that either.

## The fix

1. `InWorldScreen.watchable`: Interact on a playing screen shows the rest of the show full screen, as a cutscene. The
   cutscene starts at the line that plays now and has one shot for each line that is left. She stays where she
   stands. The world stops, as for every cutscene.
2. After the cutscene the show goes on from where the cutscene stopped. A skip of the cutscene does not skip the show.
   A cutscene that plays to the end ends the show.
3. `InWorldScreen.endComment`: at the end of the show her text shows by itself, if she is in the room. It stays for
   its reading time (about 22 s), and the Interact key closes it sooner. After the end, the Interact key on the TV
   shows it again. Before the end, nothing shows it.
4. The table has its plain line again.
5. Two placeholder stills (`tools/w14_tv_placeholders.py`): `Cutscenes/n2_speech.png` for the speech and
   `n2_credits.png` for the credits. The render of the speech replaces the first one later.

A TV that ended before a load does not play again, and after that load the key does not show her text. A load in W14
is rare, so this stays as it is.
