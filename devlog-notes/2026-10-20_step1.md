# Devlog notes, build order step 1 (2026-10-20)

For the writer: what exists after step 1, what Bruno can show on camera, what is placeholder.

## What was built

1. A new Unity 6 (6000.3.15f1, URP) project in a public GitHub repository (GPL-3.0). The Unity project sits at
   the repo root. The paid Asset Store packages (Invector x2, Gore System URP) are imported but stay out of git
   and out of MEGA.
2. The menu loop, ported from Project Warspite and cut to size: main menu, pause menu, options with a key remap
   (13 tank-control actions), a difficulty panel with three presets (EASY one shooter, NORMAL two, HARD all) plus
   three free modifiers, and the death screen in the Arkham shape (a picture, a hint, a voice line slot).
3. The training room greybox: a 10 x 10 m floor, four walls, one fixed camera, a capsule as Lucy, a kill volume.
4. The batch harness: `tools/unity.sh` (compile, tests, exec, import, play, shots, sanity), run folders with a
   log triage, 27 EditMode tests, 6 PlayMode tests, and a bot that plays the menu loop and saves screenshots.
5. The gore glue and the Invector bridge from Warspite, so step 3 can wire the dummy enemy.
