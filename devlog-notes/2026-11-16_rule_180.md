## The rule

`Automation/ScreenDirection.cs` holds the rule, for the builder and the test:

1. A grid of 0.5 m over the floor of every room. A step to the next point is a step she can walk: ground under both
   points, nothing solid at her waist, no wall between them.
2. A step that changes the camera (the zone the director picks: the highest priority) is a cut. A step through a
   doorway is a cut too.
3. On each screen, the walk has a sideways share: 1 is a full walk across the view at that depth. A walk straight at
   the lens from the side of the frame drifts sideways too, by the point's offset over its depth.
4. The cut flips when the two shares have opposite signs and both are 0.5 or more. A walk into the depth of one screen
   (a neutral shot) never flips.

The zones under a flag (a `FlagGate`, off at the start) are out of the check. A camera that turns to follow her is out
of it too: the brain eye is the only one.

## The repair in the builder

`DemoWorld.ScreenDirections` runs in `BuildAll` after every camera of the world stands:

1. Each cell camera with a flip takes a new pose (`DemoRoom.Repose`): places on every metre of its room, more aims,
   an aim along each camera on the other side of its cuts. The new pose must see every exit, every floor point that
   the old pose saw, and her chest 0.1 m in from each doorway of the cell (the doorway test looks at the screen as
   she steps through). Then the fewest flips win. Three rounds.
2. A cell camera that still flips splits its cell in two (`DemoRoom.Split`), as a cell with a blind spot does. Both
   ways to cut the cell are tried; each half must see all its floor points and exits.
3. The cameras placed by hand do not move. A flip left on one shows in the build log (`[Rule180] left:`).
4. The pass sees a fire door that starts open as open: the builder leaves its leaf shut in the doorway, and the door
   opens it in play. Without that, two doorway cuts (G2 to G5, H3b to H4) were out of the pass and in the test.

By hand, in G18: her walk north from the east bay into the north aisle went left on the bay's screen and right on the
aisle's. No one pose for the whole bay sees its floor with no flip (an offline search on the hall's colliders, every
place of the hall). So the bay has two zones: `Cam_G18_bay_east` keeps the south half, and a new `Cam_G18_bay_north`
looks straight down the north half from the north wall (yaw 180, pitch 28, lens 62): her walk north comes at the lens.

The result of the last build: 1,279 cut steps, 149 flip before the repair, 0 after, 42 camera moves, four cells split
(G19, G5 twice, H4). The demo has 209 cameras now (204 before).
