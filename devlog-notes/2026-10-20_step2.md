# Devlog notes, build order step 2 (2026-10-20)

For the writer: Lucy moves. Tank controls, fixed cameras per room, auto-lock with a target switch.

## What was built

1. Tank controls (`TankController`): W and S walk along her facing, A and D turn her, Shift runs, Space dodges.
   The input is relative to Lucy, never to the camera, so a camera cut never changes what a key does.
   Movement locks (the charge, later a stun) stop the walk but keep the facing under control.
2. The dodge: a 2.5 m dash in 0.35 s, a step back by default (forward when W is held), 0.3 s of invulnerability,
   0.8 s cooldown.
3. Fixed cameras (`FixedCamera`, `CameraZone`, `FixedCameraDirector`): each room has one wide default camera and
   zone cameras. A zone is a box. While Lucy stands in a box, its camera shows. A cut is instant, as on the PS1.
   The training room has three cameras: wide, north-east corner, south-west corner.
4. Lock-on (`LockOnController`): the nearest live target locks by itself inside 14 m and drops past 18 m or at
   death. Tab switches to the next target to the right. While the charge button is held, Lucy stands still and
   turns to face the target. A red marker floats over the target. Two capsule dummies stand in the room.
5. A status line in the top-left corner (F1 hides it): the camera, the lock target, the move state.
6. Nine PlayMode tests for the mechanics, and six new bot steps with shots of each camera and of the lock.
