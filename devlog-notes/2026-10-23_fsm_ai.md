## What I built

1. **The Invector AI body on our models.** `InvectorEnemyFactory` (editor code in
   `Scripts/Integration/Invector/Editor/`) builds a soldier the way Invector's "Create AI" window does it:
   `vControlAI` (NavMeshAgent, root motion, sight), the FSM controller, the vendor shooter animator controller,
   on the T19 model of the class (the model's own scale stays on the root). Sight: 180°, 1 to 12 m, Lucy's tag
   and layer, the room as the obstacle layer.
2. **Our FSM asset, `Assets/_Game/AI/FSM_GB_Soldier.asset`.** It opens in Invector's FSM window: Idle (find the
   target) -> Engage when the soldier sees Lucy -> Search (walk to where she was) when it loses her -> Engage
   again, or Idle at the spot. The builder writes it again on every run.
3. **"Move or shoot, never both".** The Engage state runs one custom FSM action, "Glorified Body/Move Or Shoot"
   (`GbEngageAction`), that hands the fight to `SoldierBrain` (our namespace, no Invector types):
   - Move: the gun holds fire, the body walks to a firing spot 6 m from Lucy, up to 50° off to one side, for 1.5
     to 3 s or until it arrives.
   - Shoot: the body stops. When it stands still, the gun (`DummyShooter`, the same telegraph as the training
     dummies) may aim: the laser, the lock, the circle, the shot. It needs a clear line of sight, the range
     (10 m), and a difficulty slot. After the shot, or 3 s with no aim, it moves again.
   - The feet are braced (kinematic) during the aim, so a walking soldier cannot push another off its spot.
4. **The powers on the soldiers.** A blast staggers it in place for the stagger time. A lift suspends it (no FSM,
   no agent, no physics), it hangs and turns with her as the dummies do, and after the drop it stands up and
   fights again. A slam hurts it. The power armour is heavy: "TOO HEAVY". The short hits the nearest one.
5. **Death.** Our Health is the real one. Our death kills the Invector body (its death animation). Invector's
   own clean-up is off: it destroys every MonoBehaviour on the object, ours too. After 1.5 s the corpse lies
   still with no agent, no physics and no capsule. The kill counts for the SHORT bar.
6. **Near awareness (replaced at midday, see below).** Within 6 m and with a clear line, a soldier kept track of
   Lucy out of its field of view: a flanking soldier walked past her facing away, lost her, and stood facing a
   wall.
7. **The AI TEST room** (`AiTest.unity`, `AiTestSetup.BuildAiTest`, the AI TEST button in the main menu): an 18 x
   14 m greybox hall, two low covers and two pillars, a baked NavMesh, Lucy at the south end, three soldiers at
   the north end: `Soldier_A` (soldier, 80 HP), `Guard_B` (guard, 60 HP), `Armour_C` (power armour, 250 HP,
   heavy). Two fixed cameras. No baked light (a test room).

All numbers are PROPOSED. Rule 6 holds: only `GlorifiedBody.Integration.Invector` uses Invector types.

## Bugs found on the way (all fixed)

1. Invector's "in destination" flag reads true at once after a new path: the brain walked zero steps. The brain
   now checks its own distance to the spot, after 0.6 s.
2. The walk animation carries the body for a moment after the stop: the gun now waits until the body stands.
3. The body moves on physics steps, not every frame: its speed is measured over 0.12 s.
4. A new target in Invector restarts its path: a stopped soldier walked during its aim (624 frames). The
   awareness check now leaves a stopped body alone.
5. Invector's clean-up after death destroyed our Health. It is off now.

## Midday: the guns, the shot effects, and the awareness

Bruno (2026-10-23, 12:30): "add the guns, bullets, effects (muzzle, smoke, impact), sounds of it; add the
awareness: on projectr warspite, the enemies weren't this dumb".

1. **What Warspite did** (a read of its code): the enemies were omniscient. Once they had the player, a fixed
   Invector target went back on her whenever Invector lost her (`EnemyLogic.SyncInvectorTarget`). Its guns were
   Invector's `AI_Handgun` on the right hand. A shot played a muzzle prefab, smoke, a light and the vendor fire
   clips, and wall hits spawned the vendor impact prefabs.
2. **The awareness now** (PROPOSED): a soldier is unaware until it sees Lucy, takes a hit, hears a power hit
   within 12 m, or hears an alert (`EnemyAlert`: a fellow soldier's sight, hit or shot). Then every soldier
   within 25 m is alerted for the rest of the room: it keeps her as a fixed target (Warspite's rule), it knows
   where she is, and it walks to a firing spot with a clear line to her chest (eight directions, two ranges, the
   nearest clear spot). The FSM: Idle -> Engage on "Glorified Body/Is Alerted" (`GbAlertedDecision`). The 6 m
   near-awareness rule of the night is gone.
3. **The gun** (`EnemyGun`): the vendor `AI_Handgun` with its scripts and colliders removed. The hand bones of
   our T19 rigs do not match the vendor grip, so the gun follows the right hand every frame and turns so its
   muzzle points along the aim. The bullet leaves the muzzle. The vendor animator gets the pistol set
   (`UpperBody_ID` 1), the aim pose (`IsAiming`) and the shot trigger.
4. **The shot**: the gun's own flash particles, its smoke, a 0.06 s light, a fire sound (four vendor clips, pitch
   0.95 to 1.05), an aim click when the laser starts. `ShotEffects` (`Resources/Effects/shot_effects.asset`)
   holds the clips and the impact.
5. **The bullet**: a small bright ball with a 0.12 s tracer trail. A miss hits the room with the vendor
   `impactConcrete` (bits, dust, chunks, its own sound). The training dummies' bullets get the trail and the
   impact too.

Proof: `EnemyAiTests` 8 of 8 (new: `Alert_AllFight_AndTheyKeepHerAsTarget_OutOfSight`,
`Gun_InTheHand_Fires_WithFlashAndSound_AMissHitsTheWall`). The "ai" bot 11 of 11, with a close shot of a flash
and of a wall impact. In one run the guard saw her and the other two heard its alert.
