## What changed

1. **The names, on screen only.** The bar is MK. The powers show as mk_warspite (the blast), mk_erebus (the fire)
   and mk_orzabal (the telekinesis): the HUD power line, the remap rows ("Power: mk_warspite (blast)"), the
   tutorial hints, How to Play, the difficulty text, the drug words. The code keeps Blast, Fire, Telekinesis and
   Nalcon, so the save keys and the tests stay the same. The drug NALCON keeps its name. mk_edgar (possession)
   comes later.
2. **The slam costs** a full charge of MK (no change, his "slam should cost"). Her own blast on a floater does not
   release it (no change, his "keep it that way").
3. **Targeting.** mk_orzabal lifts the enemy directly, as before. The props are a choice, not the default:
   - the auto-lock never picks a prop, also when the prop is nearer than the enemy;
   - with mk_orzabal selected, the target switch (Tab) also reaches the props;
   - with another power selected, a prop lock drops and the auto-lock takes an enemy again;
   - a thrown prop hurts the enemy it hits (the power's damage), and after the throw the lock goes back to an
     enemy;
   - a heavy target refuses the lift at no cost: the HUD shows "TOO HEAVY" for 1.5 s. The power armour becomes
     the first heavy enemy when the FSM AI arrives. Against it: the bullet catch, the thrown props, and the
     other two powers.
   The HUD also shows "NOT ENOUGH MK" for 1.5 s when a power has no MK to spend.
4. **The crates** (`PropAssets.Crate`): a 0.5 m cube, a solid collider, a lock target marked as a prop, no HP. Unlit,
   as his ruling for "objects you can throw" (§12.3): the placeholder texture (`tools/prop_crate_texture.py`) has
   a dark frame, so the edges of the cube show without light. Two in the training room, one in the gym between
   the bars and the first target. A crate lands where it hits and can be lifted again.
5. **The tutorial** has 19 steps: a prop step after the slam ("Faculty three moves objects too. Lift the crate and
   throw it into a dummy."). It ends when a prop flies.
6. **The drive fix.** The walk to the door now goes through the lane west of the bars. The first run of this
   step found the real cause of the stops near the bars: two walks in a row used two key hands, and the second
   pressed the turn key again inside the 0.4 s double-tap window. That press was a dodge (an event "dodge left"
   in the scan step). The key hands now share the release times.
