## 1. A colleague's death (§11.4: "react with shock and dismay")

`EnemyMorale` on every FSM soldier. When an enemy dies, it tells the room one frame later (by then the killing
blow's hit event carried the cause). Every live enemy that sees the corpse (a clear line within 15 m, or within
5 m) reacts:

- **Shock:** it stops and loses its aim for 1 s (2 s for a gruesome death), flinches (the vendor hit reaction) and
  barks a placeholder line over its head (`Bark`: "OH GOD...", "JESUS!", "MAN DOWN!"; the writer owns the words).
  The shock is an opening for Lucy.
- **Gruesome:** the short's shock, the telekinesis (the slam, a throw, a returned bullet, grenade or rocket), an
  explosion. A plain mk_warspite kill and a fire kill are not gruesome.

## 2. The retreat by rank (§11.5)

Fear: +1 for a death seen, +2 for a gruesome one. The limit by rank: an unarmed civilian 0 (it runs at once), an
armed civilian and a guard 3, a soldier and the heavies never. At the limit it barks "FALL BACK!", sprints to the
NavMesh spot farthest from Lucy (up to 14 m), and cowers there in a crouch with its back to her. It never attacks
again in the room. In the AI TEST room only `Guard_B` (the shield guard) can run.

## 3. The care of a body: the drag and the CPR

`CorpseCare` on every soldier but the heavies. The rule: a colleague lies dead within 15 m, and this soldier has
had no line of sight to Lucy for 3 s (she hides). The nearest free soldier runs to the body ("MAN DOWN!"), drags
it 4 m away from her (crouched, walking backward with the vendor crouch strafe, the body's chest at his hands),
then kneels at its chest and does CPR for 12 s ("STAY WITH ME!"). The CPR never brings the colleague back. When he
sees her, or anything hurts him, he drops the body and fights. One carer per body.

The animations are placeholders. No CPR or drag clip exists in the vendor set or in the RFab motion presets (103
names, RFab gap 13). The drag is the vendor crouch strafe backward. The CPR is the vendor crouch held, the upper
body pumping at 110 a minute and both arms pointed at the chest by code.
