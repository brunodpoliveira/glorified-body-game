## What changed

| Rank | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Soldier, power armour | A random side, near his own line to her. No side shared. | Claims a side of the fight (`Enemy/FlankBoard.cs`) and prefers spots out of her view. |
| Guard | A random side | Keeps to his own line to her (head-on), cover on that line only |
| Knife (scientist) | Ran straight at her | Runs to a point 2 m beside her, then strikes |
| Armed scientist (pistol) | A random side | No change |

The sides of a fight:

1. The first flanker fixes the axis: the line from her to him (the front of the fight).
2. Each flanker claims a free side of that axis, in groups: a flank (the one nearest his bearing), then the other flank,
   then her back, then the back corners, then the front corners.
3. So the second flanker takes the side opposite the first (the pinch), and a third goes behind her.
4. A claim goes at a death or a retreat. With no claim left, the axis goes too.

The spots on a side:

- A flanker circles her at range toward his side. Each move turns him at most 60 degrees around her, so his path stays
  about 5 m from her. One straight walk to the far side passed her at arm's reach, and he stopped to strike in melee.
- Around that step he tries up to 40 degrees each way, at 6 m and at 4 m (never nearer than his weapon fires). He needs
  a clear line from the muzzle to her chest.
- A spot inside her view (60 degrees each side of her facing) costs 8 m more. So a spot in her view wins only when no
  other spot exists. PROPOSED numbers.
- Cover: a flanker takes a barricade only on his side (50 degrees). A guard takes one only on his line (30 degrees).
- "Move or shoot, never both" stays: the side choice only changes where he walks before he stops to aim.

Invector has no flank, squad or attack-token system (checked 2026-11-02), so all of it is our code behind `IEnemyBody`.
