## What it is

`Tutorial.unity`: the therapy gym of the second look test with its door shut (the shared builder
`LookTestSetup.BuildTherapyGym`), the baked bounce, three fixed cameras (the door corner by default, the corner
over the rack for the north band, the west wall for the strip by the door), Lucy 3 m from the door camera, three
padded targets in a row across the middle (canvas, a vest, a head; Health 150, a lock point, the Enemy layer),
a fourth against the west wall by the door with a gun (a DummyShooter off and out of the lock until its step),
a capsule by the bars. The main menu has a TUTORIAL button under START.

`TutorialDirector` runs the steps in order. Each step is the presenter's line (a placeholder until the writer
gives the words), a control hint with {Action} tokens, and a condition on what the player did (the events of
TankController, LockOnController, PsychicPowers, ShortController). `TutorialPromptUI` shows the step count,
the line and the hint with the bound keys (the remap changes the words) at the bottom of the screen. F2 skips a
step (debug). The last step completes the level (the level complete overlay: restart or the main menu).

| # | Step | The presenter (placeholder) | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | walk | "Subject, to the bars, please." | she stands within 1.3 m of the bars |
| 2 | run | "Now to the monitors. At a run." | she ran during the step and stands by the monitors |
| 3 | dodge | "Something comes at you. Step aside." | a dodge to the left or the right |
| 4 | switch | "The lock finds the nearest dummy by itself. Choose another one." | the target changed |
| 5 | blast | "Faculty one. Fill the circle on a dummy and release." | BLAST fired at a full circle |
| 6 | fire | "Faculty two. Only a full circle burns." | FIRE fired at a full circle |
| 7 | lift_throw | "Faculty three. Lift one, turn, and throw it into another." | a throw |
| 8 | catch | "The dummy by the door has a gun. ... catch the bullet and send it back." | a bullet caught (the gun is on for this step only) |
| 9 | capsule | "A capsule lies by the bars. Take it. Use it." | a drug used |
| 10 | short | "The overflow. Your bar is full: the next faculty shorts. A capsule ends it." | the short began and ended (the director fills the bar and gives a capsule) |
| 11 | complete | "Demonstration complete. Thank you, ladies and gentlemen." | 2.5 s, then the level completes |

The later features add their steps here: the log (read a log), the scan (an image, a video), the props (lift
a prop), the enemies.
