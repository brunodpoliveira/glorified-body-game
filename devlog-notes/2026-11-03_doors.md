# 2026-11-03: fire doors at chokepoints, the DOOR TEST room

Bruno:

```text
doors should be lockable by the player as well to stop chases and breachable by the soldier and power armor enemies (so
non-critical doors (i.e ones that don't require cards or puzzles to be solved) can be damaged and destroyed by the
soldiers but not by scientists and guards, they can bang on the doors though); strategic use of their properties will
be key for a player to succeed
this will need a guess what? a new dev test room, that's right
```

The first build gave every doorway a closed door (his rule of the morning: "a door is default closed and opens when
someone interacts with it"). His answer after my report:

```text
I was thinking that it'll massively slow the player down to have to interact with each and every door this way, or have
the possibility of doign so would break the game something fierce; it needs to be only certain doors that can open and
close in selected chokepoints on the map; maybe it would be better if there was a lever that closes+ locks/jams the door
shut (justified by them being firebreaks, that's why guards can't open them even if they engage the lever on the other
side). if the player doesn't want to engage in running, wants to fight on they can do just that. or if they wanna ignore
this feature, they can do that too
```

So a normal doorway has no door. A fire door stands at a chokepoint only.

His answers to my three questions (the same day):

```text
1: agreed
2: yes; add a horizontal bar to indicate that she is jamming the door in some way that prevents it from moving; we'll add better graphics later
3: her powers can break a door as well, although there would be little reason for a player to deliberately break a door; but it should be an option
```

1. The demo has three fire doors: W2 to W5 (the two ward corridors), W5 to W7 (the corridor to the gym), G2 to G5 (the

## What a fire door does (`Interaction/FireDoor.cs`, `Interaction/DoorLever.cs`)

| State | Her lever | An enemy after her, on the other side |
|---|---|---|
| Open (the start) | Shuts and jams the door. "Something is in the way." when a body stands in the doorway. | Walks through. |
| Jammed | Opens the door again. | Stops at the door. A soldier kicks (25 HP, every 1.2 s). The power armour hits (50 HP, every 1.6 s). A guard or a scientist bangs (no damage, about every 1 s). |
| Broken | Nothing. A hole stays. | Walks through. |

- A lever stands on each side of the doorway, at hand height. The red handle points up when the door is open and down
  when it is jammed. The enemies never pull a lever.
- A yellow bar on two brackets lies across each face while the door is jammed.
- The leaf has 100 HP (`Destruction/DoorLeaf.cs`, a breakable). Four soldier kicks or two armour blows break it. The
  pieces fall where the leaf stood. A broken door stays broken (the damage ledger, the saves).
- An explosion (a grenade, a rocket) and her powers also break a jammed door. Her powers reach it by their splash: a
  blast near the door, a thrown crate or body. The enemy bullets and the short do not (PROPOSED).
- A jammed leaf stops her, the enemies, the bullets and the sight. The NavMesh goes through the doorway, so a chaser
  walks up to the leaf.
- Card doors and puzzle doors stay `LockedDoor`: nothing breaks them. They carve the NavMesh, so no enemy tries.
- Sounds: `door_kick` and `door_bang` (placeholders, `tools/door_sfx.py`). The leaf shakes on each hit. The enemy
  swings its arms (the vendor melee clip).
- The save keeps each jammed door (`RoomState.jammed`).
- The enemy side: `Enemy/IDoorWaiter.cs`. `SoldierBrain` stands, faces the door and swings while the door holds it.
  An unarmed civilian has no door rule: a jammed door only stops it. No test covers the civilians yet.
- A room spec builds one with `new Door(..., fire: true, pocket: 1 or -1)` (`BreakableAssets.Door`). The room that
  lists the doorway first builds the door. `pocket` picks the wall side the leaf slides into: a doorway near a corner
  has wall on one side only (W2 to W5).

## Three faults found on the way (each with a test that failed first)

1. The card doors stood in the wrong place since 2026-10-31. `DemoRoom.LockedLeaf` put the leaf at twice its doorway's
   position (a local position under a parent at the doorway). So the W12 stair card door did not stop her. The closet
   soldier of DOOR TEST saw her through the empty doorway. Fixed. Tests: `DemoTests.TheStair_...` (the leaf stands in
   W12's doorway) and `DoorTests.NothingBreaksACardDoor`.
2. The same fault put the CRT bodies of `DemoRoom.CrtWall` 34.7 m from their screen faces (W7 and the other CRTs).
   Fixed. Test: `DemoTests.EveryCrt_StandsBehindItsFace`.
3. Her lock stayed on a target behind a door that shut. In the world rooms a held target now drops after 1 s out of
   her sight (`LockOnController.LostSightSeconds`, PROPOSED). Test: `DoorTests.AJammedDoor_BetweenHerAndHerTarget_DropsTheLock`.

The menu: 13 test rooms ran past the bottom of the screen. The test buttons are shorter now.
