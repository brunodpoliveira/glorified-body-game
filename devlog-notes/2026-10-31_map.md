## What it does

| Part | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| The key | M opens the map (`InputManager.GameAction.Map`, remappable). The room stops (`GameState.Map`), as for the inventory. M, Back or Escape closes it. |
| Hidden at the start | The map shows only the rooms she knows: the room she is in, and every room she was in before. |
| Room by room | A room shows when she enters it, and it stays. |
| The floor plan | A key item of the new kind `Map` (no slot). While she carries it, the whole floor shows. |
| Key places | Locked doors (red, green once open), items (yellow), logs (white), save machines (blue). A taken item goes off the map, in this room and in the rooms she left. A carried item (a key card on a lab worker) is not a key place. |
| Her place | A pink square with a line the way she faces. Only on her own floor. |
| Floors | The map opens on her floor. A and D change the floor. Only a floor with a known room is in the list. |
| Doors out | A small white diamond at each exit. |
| Saves | The map needs no save data of its own. It reads the rooms she left (`WorldState`, in the saves) and her items. A new game from the menu clears it. |
| Inventory | The floor plan says "The whole floor shows on the map. Press M to see it." |
| Tutorial | A new step "map" after "inventory": open the map, close it. The tutorial has 24 steps now. |

## How it works

- `Scripts/Map/MapAtlas.cs`: the floors and the rooms. A room has its floor pieces, its exits and its key places, read from its scene. The game loads it from `Resources/Map/map_atlas.asset`.
- `Scripts/Map/MapKnowledge.cs`: what she knows (a room, a floor plan) and the state of each key place.
- `Scripts/Map/MapScreen.cs`: the screen. One object for the whole game ("[Map]"), so no room needed a rebuild.
- `Editor/MapAtlasSetup.cs`: the layout table (which scene lies on which floor, and where) and the bake. It opens each scene and reads every object named "...Floor", every `RoomExit`, `LockedDoor`, `ItemPickup` and `SavePoint`. Run it after a room builder changes a room: `tools/unity.sh exec GlorifiedBody.Editor.MapAtlasSetup.BuildAtlas`.
- A demo room needs two things: a floor piece named "...Floor", and a line in the layout table.
