# 2026-10-22: the pick, and the LOOK option

Bruno (2026-10-22, 14:00, with a screenshot of the gym in the baked dressing): "real time with the baked bounce
looks the best; the pure plates lose char shadows and aren't that much better image-wise to make it worth it.
skylight shafts: straight down. render scale up to 1080. crossed cutout cards fine. would like to have an
option for max quality and aliasing; some players might want pixel clarity, others the authentic look. remember
that we want the vibes of the look, but we don't need to be clamped by the same tech limitations; we can
'cheat' to make the picture look better".

## What changed

1. The look of the game is real time with the baked bounce: hard spots live, the lightmaps carry the indirect
   light and the ambient occlusion, the probes light the figures. Every look room opens in that dressing
   (`LookDirector.Configure` takes the start dressing; the serialized value of the old scenes is replaced). The
   plate dressing stays in the look test as a reference and is not in the game. A game room needs its bake
   (`LookTestSetup.BakeAll` is the model).
2. The frame is 1080p: `LookDirector.LookRenderScale` is 1 (720p of 1080p before). The plate, when used, is
   1920 x 1080 as well.
3. The LOOK option, Options > Display, a two-way row: Authentic (1999) or Clear (anti-aliasing).
   `SettingsManager.LookQuality` keeps it in PlayerPrefs (`settings_lookQuality`) and raises
   `OnLookQualityChanged`. `LookQualityApplier` on "[GameplaySystems]" of every playable room applies it at
   Start and on a change: Authentic = GB_Full (the stencil mark and the 32-level dither on the background),
   MSAA off, no camera anti-aliasing; Clear = the stock renderer (no dither), MSAA 4x on the pipeline, SMAA
   high on the camera. In a look room `LookDirector` owns the renderer and reads the quality for its live
   dressings (the plate keeps GB_Plate). Both looks keep the hard light, the bounce, the 2K textures, the fixed
   cameras: the vibe. The resolution, the anti-aliasing and the filtering are the cheats.
4. Lucy is on the Player layer and every figure on the Enemy layer in every room, from the shared builders
   (`MenuLoopSetup.SetLayer` in EnsureLucy, EnsureDummy, EnsureCastFigure), so the dither skips the characters
   in the training room too. The physics matrix allows every pair (checked in `DynamicsManager.asset`).
5. The skylight shafts of the corridor point straight down. The ferns stay crossed cutout cards.

## Proof

  no anti-aliasing, Lucy on Player and the dummies on Enemy; Clear puts the stock renderer with MSAA 4x and

- PlayMode `LookRoomTests` (both look rooms): the room opens in RealTimeBaked at scale 1; under Clear the live
  dressings use the stock renderer and keep the bounce, the plate keeps its renderer, Authentic brings GB_Full
  back.
- EditMode `LookTestAssetsTests`: the render scale is 1 and MSAA is off on disk.
