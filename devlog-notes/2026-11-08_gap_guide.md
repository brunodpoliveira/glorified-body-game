# The gap of G16 guides a crate (2026-11-08, night)

## The fault

`DemoTests.TheGap_SheCannotCross_ACrateFillsIt_ThenSheWalksOver` passed at 23:54 and at 00:31. It then failed in three runs after 02:54. The crate rested 1 m high, on the rim of the gap, 0.17 m east of where it fell in before.

- The commit `e32cba9` passed at 00:31, and the same code fails now. So the cause is not in the night's commits.
- The saved settings are the defaults (mouse sensitivity 3).
- The gap is 1.2 m wide and the crate 1.1 m. The crate has 5 cm of room on each side. The test steers the crate to within 5 cm of the centre, and the carry moves it once each frame. A small change of the frame timing can put it on the rim. I did not measure the frame times of the runs. So that last step is my reading, not a proof.

The same 5 cm is a fault for the player too. A crate that the mouse places must land within 5 cm.

## The fix

`PitReturn` guides a crate. A free crate at rest within 0.45 m of the gap's edge slides to the centre line of the gap, square to it, and falls in. The guide does not touch a held crate, or one that still moves faster than 0.5 m/s. The 0.45 m is PROPOSED.
