1. The game opens on the cutscene.
   - The beat `W1_Wake` is black from the first frame (`ScriptedSequence.startsBlack`, `ScreenFade.Cover`). The 1 s wait
     is gone.
   - `CutscenePlayer` does not fade out a screen that is already black. The cutscene fades in from black.
   - A load past this beat stays clear: the check runs in `Start`, after the flags of the load stand.
2. The scientist meets her when she leaves the room.
   - The trigger stands in the corridor W2, just past the door (z 13 to 13.6). The door is the only way out of W1, and the
     trigger is wider than the door.
   - He waits at the west end of the corridor from the start, so he does not pop into view. She stops, he walks to her,
     the cutscene plays, he steps closer and attacks.
   - The three pistol scientists of W2 stand in the same corridor. Their sight alert would break the talk, so they wait
     until it ends (`EnemySpawn.heldUntilFlag`, `SoldierBrain.ScriptHoldUntil`). The last step alerts the knife scientist,
     and that alert calls them. A hit or a power near them wakes them earlier. A load past the flag lets them go at once.
