# 2026-10-26, night: the room kit, enemies by marker, one room with everything

The design sweep's rank 5: no repeatable way to build a room, and every room with Invector enemies had to stay out
of the public repo. The scene held the figures with the unpacked vendor weapons and shield. The gaps sweep: no room had
soldiers, civilians, cover, items and a save machine together.

## What changes

| Part | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Enemy prefabs | `InvectorEnemyFactory.BuildEnemyPrefabs` builds eight kinds as prefabs in `Resources/Enemies/`: `soldier_pistol`, `soldier_rifle`, `soldier_gas`, `guard_pistol`, `guard_shield`, `power_armour`, `labworker`, `nurse`. The folder is git-ignored (vendor data). Build it once after the vendor import. |
| Spawn markers | `EnemySpawn`: the scene holds only a marker (the kind, a civilian's pose variant). At the room's load the marker becomes its figure, with the same name, parent and place. So the saves find it by the same path, and the scene holds no vendor data. A clone with no prefabs logs one line for each marker. |
| Room kit | `RoomKit` (editor): static boxes, the NavMesh bake, spawn markers, exits, entries, sequences, triggers. |
| Camera check | `CameraCoverage` reads a kit room's box floors when the room has no `RoomFace` floor. |
| The three test rooms | AI TEST, COVER TEST and CIVILIAN TEST use markers now. |

## The KIT TEST room

`KitTest` (`KitTestSetup.BuildKitTest`), in git: a 20 x 14 m hall.

- Three armed figures (a rifle, a pistol, a guard with a shield) and two civilians (a lab worker, a nurse).
- Two barricades and a table, a pillar, a NALCON pill, a save machine, a checkpoint.
- A door west to TRAVEL B.
- Its own music key (`kit_hall`).
- Three cameras: each half of the hall from the far wall, and one at the door for the corridor. The coverage test
  placed them. A camera behind the south wall lost the south strip to the wall. A camera close inside it lost the
  sides. The pillar moved to a corner, where its shadow hides little floor.

## Proof

`KitTests` (5):

- The markers become their figures in place, with their brains and the guard's shield, at the save's paths. No
  marker stays, no prefab is missing, the NavMesh is there, the music key is the room's.
- Every floor point is in the frame of its camera: 5 of 1034 out, under the 5 % bar.
- A fight: the soldiers hunt her and the music turns. Her three kills pay 55 exp, the lab worker flees, and the
  ambient comes back.
- A save and a load keep the dead soldier dead and the pill taken, and spawn no second figure.
- A trip to TRAVEL B and back keeps the dead soldier dead, with no second figure.

The three converted rooms: every test that uses them passes on the spawned figures. The full run: EditMode 49/49,
PlayMode 227/227, the six bots CLEAN (the ai, cover and civilians bots run in the converted rooms).

A scene check: the vendor folders hold 3296 asset ids. AiTest, CoverTest, CivilianTest and KitTest refer to none of
them (TrainingRoom also 0, as a control). The AI room's scene fell to 80 KB.
