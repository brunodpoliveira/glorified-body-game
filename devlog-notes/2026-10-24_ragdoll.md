## 1. The ragdoll

- **The bones:** `HumanoidRagdoll` (editor code) puts 11 bodies on each soldier: a box on the hips and on the chest,
  a sphere on the head, capsules on the thighs, shins, upper arms and forearms, and CharacterJoints. Each joint opens
  toward the side a body bends (the knee back, the elbow and the hip forward). These are the parts of Unity's
  ragdoll wizard, which does not run outside its window in batch mode. `InvectorEnemyFactory.BuildRagdoll` calls
  it for every soldier except the heavies. The bones are on the BodyPart layer: they hit the room and other bodies,
  not Lucy and not the capsules.
- **The states** (`EnemyRagdoll`): Animated, Held, Down, StandingUp, Corpse. While he is animated, the bones are
  kinematic and their colliders are off, so nothing else in the game changes. `SoldierBrain`, the gun and the
  shield treat a body that is down like a lifted body (`IRagdollBody`, `RagdollBodies.IsDown`).
- **The stand-up:** Invector's own method. The vendor clip `StandUp@FromBack` or `StandUp@FromBelly` plays, the
  root turns to match the lying body, and the bones blend from the ragdoll pose to the clip in 0.3 s.
- **A dead body** settles, then freezes in its pose. The drag of `CorpseCare` still moves it as one piece.
- A guard who cowers stays frightened after a fall.

## 2. mk_orzabal (item 4)

- The floater hangs from the hips with its legs and arms loose.
- A throw: the body flies, steers toward its target (a running soldier no longer steps out of the throw), and
  tumbles on as a ragdoll. The enemy it hits falls too, pushed along the flight. Both stay down 2.5 s, then stand
  up. A heavy only staggers.
- A slam: the body falls hard and stays down 2.5 s. A release (another enemy's hit): it drops limp, 1.2 s.
- A wall between the floater and the target still stops the throw.

## 3. mk_warspite at full charge (item 5)

- A full circle knocks the soldier down for 2.5 s, pushed away from her at 4 m/s, then he stands up. A part charge
  only staggers, as before. The power armour does not fall.

## 4. The gore (item 2)

- **The head burst** is only for a kill by the short.
- **A limb comes off** on a full-charge mk_warspite kill, and on the other gruesome kills (the telekinesis, an
  explosion, a grenade, a rocket), PROPOSED. The limb is a forearm, a whole arm or a shin, at random. The bone
  shrinks to nothing, and the vendor bone ends and meat (`goreLimb-prefab`) sit on the cut, with a gush. The
  ragdoll loses that limb's colliders. This is the cheap path of the gore study. No piece flies off: the full
  slicer needs cut models for each class (16 to 24 h).
- `HitInfo.Charge` carries the power's charge, so the morale and the gore know a full circle.
- **The blood pool** (item 6): after a death, when the hips lie still, a dark pool grows under the hips for 5 s to
  about 1.25 m (0.8 m on Gore Low, none on Off). It stays for the room, 8 at most, the oldest goes first. A
  dragged body leaves its pool behind. The materials are dark copies of the vendor pool stains (the bright hit
  stains read as red paint on a smooth pool mask).
- `GoreLimbSetup.Assign` (editor code) fills the gore profile: the stumps, the gush, the pool materials.
