## What a soldier does (`Enemy/CivilianEscort.cs`)

1. Every soldier has the escort (`SoldierBrain.Awake` adds it for the rank Soldier). Guards, the armour and the lab
   workers do not.
2. Twice a second, a soldier in a fight looks for a living civilian in his room, within 9 m of her and 8 m of him,
   and more than 2.5 m from her. Hiders, a civilian in another escort or care, and a civilian she holds do not count.
3. He starts only when another fighter within 15 m covers him, and when a spot away from her has a route
   (`SafeSpot`: 11 m from her, or as far as the room lets it, at least 3 m further than now).
4. He sprints to the civilian's side that faces her. Then the civilian walks to the spot, and he walks backward
   behind it, facing her. The rifle stays raised at his right shoulder, level, to her, and his left hand rests on the
   civilian's back (`EnemyGun.FreeHandTo`, the two-bone reach of the drag). The civilian waits when it gets 1.0 m ahead
   of him.
5. It ends at the spot, at 11 m from her, or after 16 s. A hurt, a lift, a shock, a door, a retreat or her within 1.5 m
   of the civilian ends it early (`SoldierBrain.StopCare`). Then the civilian is free again, and he goes back to the fight
   after a 20 s rest from escorts.
6. He holds his fire the whole time ("move or shoot, never both").

The civilian walks by itself: no ragdoll, as the plan proposed. The escort uses the care phase of both brains
(`ICarer.TryBeginCare`), as the corpse drag does.
