# Devlog notes, build order step 3 (2026-10-20)

For the writer: the training room fights back. The circle charge, the three powers, the three bars, the short,
the drugs, and a dummy enemy with the telegraph.

## What was built

1. The circle charge (`PsychicPowers`): keys 1, 2, 3 select BLAST, FIRE, TELEKINESIS. Hold the attack button:
   Lucy stands still, turns to the locked target, and a circle fills over her target in 1.5 s. Release: the power
   fires with that charge. Cost and damage scale with the charge. A tap below 20 percent fizzles.
2. The powers (first forms, greybox): BLAST hits at once and staggers the enemy, so its aim breaks. FIRE hits and
   leaves a burn that ticks for 4 s. TELEKINESIS lifts the enemy, holds it, and slams it into the floor. Every hit
   goes through the hit bus, so the gore glue sees it later.
3. The bars (`PsychicStats`): HP in `Health`. NALCON is what the powers spend. SHORT rises by 25 per kill and by
   0.6 per HP of damage taken. The nude outfit recharges all three by itself, the guard outfit does not (`OutfitProfile`).
4. THE SHORT (`ShortController`): a full SHORT bar turns the next release into a short. For 4 s she loses control,
   shocks hit everyone within 6 m every 0.4 s, and she loses half her HP. It never kills her. A drug ends it at once.
5. The drugs (`DrugInventory`, `DrugPickup`): one type per power (Nalcon, Red, D-Felon, names from Galerians). Any
   drug ends a short and refills HP and nalcon. Three lie on the room floor. The nude outfit carries two of each.
6. The dummy enemy (`DummyShooter`): a capsule that aims with a laser line, locks the aim, spins and shrinks a
   circle over its head for 1 s, then fires a bullet at the locked point. The difficulty preset limits how many
   enemies aim at once (`ShooterScheduler`: easy 1, normal 2, hard all). Killed by the shooter, the death screen
   knows the killer and shows that enemy's hint (placeholder words).
7. The HUD (`PlayerHud`): three bars bottom-left, the powers and the drug counts bottom-right, the charge circle
   over the target, the SHORT warning. Greybox art.
8. Fifteen PlayMode tests for the mechanics, six new bot steps with shots.

## Scope decision (PROPOSED, Bruno decides)

The dummy enemy is our own capsule shooter, not yet an Invector FSM AI character. The telegraph, the slots and the
death hint are game rules and belong to us. The Invector character (animations, rig, cover AI) comes with the
character models from RFab, after the look test. The MIS refactor of the Invector scripts waits for that.
